# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific and constructive feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- protecting private and security-sensitive information;
- keeping technical discussions relevant and evidence-based.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate intimidation or disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- exploiting project infrastructure or other participants;
- retaliating against good-faith reports.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-managed communication spaces, and public interactions made in an official project capacity.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a verified contact channel. Reports will be reviewed with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants when behavior is inconsistent with this policy.

## Attribution

This is a concise project-specific conduct policy. It may be replaced by a formally adopted community standard as the contributor community grows.
